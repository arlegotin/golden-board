Look at the examples, make a selection, and see what follows.

Outlined areas can be selected. Their small corner numbers identify the selection controls. Use Commit to send your selection, then Next to continue.

How the controls work

Reset clears your current selection. It uses one action, as does selecting or committing. You can commit an empty selection. A completed response stays fixed until you choose Next. Watch example walks through supplied actions separately from your response. Take each step at your own pace. Fit width and zoom change the display without using actions. Cells stay large enough to read; scroll inside a diagram when it cannot fit. Export whenever you want to stop; restarting exports this attempt before opening a new one.
