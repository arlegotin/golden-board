# Revised source-side semantic damage oracle

This owner governs the independent expected semantic projection used while
building new M2 evidence. It does not award a gate. Full canonical result
admission additionally requires independently derived route hypotheses,
global attempt/VM/adapter resources and exact Python/Rust result equality.
Those dimensions must not be replaced by copied production-decoder outputs.

The oracle may receive the source-built carrier, its exact section/group
ownership and one owned observation from `damage-corpus-v2.md`. A production
receiver may receive none of that source context. The oracle must not call a
production receiver or accept saved results. It extracts actual observed
units at the source-declared placement, normalizing the owned D0 view only;
OBS_UNITS uses physical IDs, never arrival order. Known clean equality can
avoid redundant neutral codec work in this source-side oracle only. Decoded
damaged data and exposed streams must still come from observation bytes.
Before applying a construction-specific expected rejection or D0 normalization,
bind the complete supplied case metadata and raw observation to a freshly
source-generated owned case. A caller-supplied family/ordinal alone is not a
proof that the corresponding construction occurred. Deliberately reauthored
unowned test probes may exercise private semantic helpers but cannot enter
the owned corpus projection.

Retain the inherited v1 source oracle's complete lane/raw-repetition candidate
union, full-block deduplication, conflict rejection, group identity checks,
eligible section check attempts and checked section projection. Substitute
only the explicit profile8 tuple, inventory2, physical groups and bounded
body codec1. Required/all body IDs come from the checked tier frames; each
complete assembled stream undergoes full content-v0 admission. Invalid
required content exposes neither stream. Invalid all-only content may leave
valid required content available. Matching checks alone establish neither
inventory nor typed content.

Group ambiguity and per-input diagnostics are distinct. For locally valid
lanes A′/A/A/A/A, the group is ambiguous and cannot supply a chosen fragment;
the section is corrupt unless an independent complete checked candidate
exists. Each input row retains its own uniquely checked lane state and common
block hash. The inherited `expected_fragment_state = "ambiguous"` names
the group result, not a rewrite of all five lane rows. Repetition candidates
have no synthetic input row. A checked section containing a malformed body
codec or tier remains checked in the section projection; typed content
admission independently controls stream availability and artifact state.

Use profile8 for active grouping. When its inventory cannot be established,
retain only individually checked diagnostic sections and observed lane facts;
source ownership is not permission to invent a catalog. The diagnostic
registry is8,2,3,4,5,6,7. Bootstrap IDs1..5 retain their physical replica
meaning; other IDs without inventory have unknown grouping. A foreign
profile cannot establish the profile8 artifact or its content tiers.

For this explicit v2 projection, a well-framed OBS_UNITS observation admits the
active registry's bootstrap procedure; a complete admitted square route admits
that route's bootstrap procedure. This establishes the fixed five physical
positions1..5 even if every local check fails or every position is absent.
Thus every present ID1..5 has replica_index=id−1 and physical_replica_count=5,
including a lane with a checked foreign-profile header. Its semantic identity
and common-block hash still come only from that lane's own unique local check;
invalid lanes have absent semantic identity and zero hash. An omitted fixed
position has a missing row naming the expected profile8/section1/copy0/fragment0
and its fixed replica fields, with zero hash. This is protocol-fixed physical
layout, not successful inventory or profile recovery. Other missing identities
cannot be invented without a checked inventory. Historical schemas0/1 retain
their existing owners and outputs.

The accidental-damage wrong-accept baseline is the named clean candidate.
Count each checked canonical section envelope differing from that source
once, even if both content streams remain equal. Artifact state `exact`
alone does not satisfy this gate. The required threshold stays zero.
Reauthenticated B0 probes are explicitly separate: their changed checked
section can be a correct transport diagnostic, while its malformed typed
content must remain unavailable. Preserve the raw difference count as a
diagnostic; never mix it into D0–D7 totals or erase a failing accidental case.

For the corpus's explicit package-resource overflow observations, expected
semantic availability is empty and state is resource-limit. Oversized
geometry and the six all-sector compact-framing mutants have empty semantic
availability and failure state. This uses an owned construction proof, not
a heuristic for arbitrary input. Complete resource accounting still derives
the work before rejection. A complete valid foreign route does not itself
make an artifact ambiguous; ambiguity requires distinct complete checked
downstream results.

For square-path selection, first normalize every active-profile8 path result
by removing resource counters and accepted hypotheses, and compare it before
deduplication. A checked, admitted inventory makes a result established for
this selection even when malformed required content gives it failure state;
its checked section diagnostics remain valid. Established results take
precedence over paths without an inventory. More than one distinct established
result produces a closed ambiguous result; exactly one is selected. Without
an established result, exactly one distinct active diagnostic result may be
retained as failure with no established profile. Multiple distinct failed
diagnostic results produce a closed failure, not ambiguity. Do not arbitrarily
choose the earliest failed map or merge incompatible diagnostics. Foreign
square-path results never participate in this active-profile selection.
Accepted hypotheses and route-profile identities retain their separately
owned inclusion rules for every admitted route. OBS_UNITS has its single
shared registry path and retains its separately owned diagnostic projection.

The development semantic projection contains artifact_state, section_states,
wrong_accepts, required_stream, all_stream, reauthored_boundary and the
corresponding immutable section/fragment diagnostics. Source section rows
include unknown states when a catalog is unavailable; the visible diagnostic
projection contains only what the observation establishes. This projection
has no serialized gate-result schema and no passing-resource sentinel.
Adding the remaining dimensions requires the exact resource owner and fresh
three-way convergence before any complete damage receipt is emitted.
