# Trials and experiments

A decoder can recover correct bytes without understanding chess. A model can
answer chess questions from pretraining without learning from a message. Golden
Board investigated these separately; neither a hash match nor an articulate answer
establishes the whole intended chain.

## Three information conditions

| Condition | Supplied information | Supported question |
|---|---|---|
| A: raw reconstruction | Delimited raw0/1, position count, neutral administration and permitted generic tools | Can the recipient discover the representation and extract checked content? |
| B: interpretation | Exact A-recovered bodies through the generic mechanism, with assessment requests | Can it apply artifact-specific relations and distinguish rivals? |
| C: guided use, historical | The modern aid with English controls | Does guided display/control behavior follow the artifact? |

The complete chain is raw observation → submitted decoder → isolated rerun →
checked recovered bytes → interpretation. Replacing a failed decoder's output
with a developer-generated body breaks this chain. Native developer decoding
remains useful engineering verification but is not independent recipient discovery.

Fresh, continued, cooperative and notes/template-assisted sessions are different
conditions. A new chat does not erase pretrained knowledge. Protocol numbers are
not counts of independent experiments: some identify continuations, administration
or unrun/superseded checkpoints. No total is invented here.

## Final raw reconstruction: what was actually checked

The p0048 reconstruction used an unchanged submitted decoder in fresh
default-deny OS sandboxes,
with only each allowed observation, generic runtime and fresh output. Repository,
truth, donors, prior outputs and network access were denied.

It found a unique normalization, a64-bit legend and separator, and derived the
reflected CRC polynomial from worked shift transitions. Eight scope/init/final
candidates and both byte orders were tested. All six section boundaries and the
1395-byte zero reserve matched. Independent checks compared 32,249 decoded record
projections/locations,773,976 original boundary bits and 469 animations containing
11,445 complete states, including every anthology state. A correct final digest
was therefore accompanied by intermediate-method evidence.

All16 transformation families and the no-final-LF case had already been selftested
by the recipient. Their reruns establish finite conformance, not unseen-class
generalization. The decoder encodes the learned grammar; it is not a general
unknown-format inference system. Five particular invalid cases were refused;
arbitrary malformed-input robustness is not established for that submitted method.
Invalid runs may leave quarantined diagnostics, never accepted A-to-B content.
The production decoder has separate strict validation checks.

Complete original tool history, model/mode and exclusive original information
use could not be verified. These remain limits on attribution. Optional
prior-informed chess analysis after binary extraction supplies no new learning
or interpretation credit.

## The actual byte bridge and inherited interpretation

The [final bridge](../history/experiments.md#byte-bridge-and-interpretation) records that the five
**actual received** A bodies initialized the unchanged generic mechanism.
Developer bodies were comparison truth only. That bridge checked 4,535 frames,
9,123 successful actions,757 atomic refusals, passive routes and 103 catalog roots.
These are automated mechanism checks, not a new recipient B study.

The historical [applicability findings](../history/experiments.md#byte-bridge-and-interpretation)
reported retention of the earlier146-stratum composition through exact equality
of all five bodies, required displayed/input/prerequisite slices,188 bindings and
mechanism modules. The public summary describes the resulting scope and assistance
limits; it does not include the original per-stratum evaluation rows.
The [presentation findings](../history/experiments.md#presentation-roles) describe
the prospective scoped closure rather than retrospectively passing c0038.

The supported claim is continued, current-source, own-work/template-assisted
finite A-to-B application. Prior chess/geometric hypotheses, source feedback,
neutral routes and assessment vocabulary were admitted. Incomplete outer history
and unknown original model/mode/exclusive use remain limitations. Literal source
bindings and isolated executable derivation support the bounded findings; they do
not prove instruction-caused acquisition or absence of all original outside help.

## What remains unsupported

No result establishes human learning or retention, universal alien comprehension,
a population success rate, erased pretraining, wholly unassisted discovery,
general damaged-input repair or arbitrary-format decoding. Earlier guided browser
work concerned mediated plan/control conformance, not human usability. That aid
is now removed; no guided product is advertised here.

The public account summarizes technical outcomes and their limits in
[development findings](../history/experiments.md). It is not a complete historical
trial dataset. Current native tests and deterministic builds are independently
available from this checkout; they do not rerun historical model investigations.
Future experiments require separately declared information conditions and evidence.
