# Origins and evolution

Golden Board was conceived as a gift: teaching a small set of
rules that opens a world of human invention, sacrifice, defense and beauty. It
names the Voyager Golden Record, Arecibo message, Cosmic Call, Lincos and CosmicOS
as inspirations.

The earliest surviving materials, dated **5 November 2026**, contain an operating
guide and roadmap rather than executable machinery. No earlier prototype is
established by the surviving evidence.

## From requirements to a connected slice

The initial mission already joined three difficult tasks: raw decoding, practical
chess instruction and complete game records. It also required recovery after
damage and a modern offline aid. These earlier requirements explain code that
later disappeared; they should not be read as current guarantees.

The first implementation established the pinned environment, source accounting
and legal-rule boundary. The inert document/player mechanism followed, connecting
teaching, source-game playback and a first experimental square. Small fixtures proved that components could connect; they
did not establish that the full curriculum or anthology fit.

A first interpretation relay displayed one requested frame per manual return
cycle. After eight cycles, basic prerequisite inspection had consumed substantial
operator effort without reaching an assessment. The [stopped-relay analysis](../history/experiments.md#relay-and-passive-teaching)
identifies a delivery-method problem; it does not conclude that the teaching was
unintelligible. That path was stopped and replaced with complete passive exports.
[p0005](../history/experiments.md#relay-and-passive-teaching) supported three finite
source-bound cases in a continued context.
It remained developmental, prior-informed evidence.

## The recovery branch

The original carrier used separated replicated blocks, conflict detection,
normalization and recovery instruction. A sequence of actual investigations
found different gaps: orientation/polarity normalization, conflict handling,
checked availability, numeric windows and integrated global reconstruction.
Partial successes did not automatically compose.

Cooperative sessions later combined recipient-created discoveries successfully,
as recorded in the [actual integration analysis](../history/experiments.md#recovery-and-capacity).
Recovery was **not proved impossible**. The later clean-input contract changed
the supported scope; it does not imply that every recovery attempt failed.

On **9 November**, the design adopted [clean single-copy storage](../history/experiments.md#clean-input-scope).
The clean-input contract excludes damaged-input guarantees, replicas, voting
and recovery-only teaching. All 64 games, the complete practical curriculum and the 2048² maximum
remained. Four recovery-only obligations disappeared: 150 became 146. The mixed
p0023 checkpoint was canceled/superseded, never passed. Historical damaged-input
results neither qualify nor block the final clean-input contract.

## Field identity and composed prerequisites

The [p0011 analysis](../history/experiments.md#field-windows-and-composed-prerequisites) showed that lossless
regrouping and correct recovered values did not establish the intended field
windows: zero edge bits and correlated widths admitted a shifted-window rival.
An actual reference started one cell too early. This drove explicit field extents,
reference counts and independently varied quantities rather than more copies of
an ambiguous example.

The [c0017 foundation analysis](../history/experiments.md#field-windows-and-composed-prerequisites)
reported five exact commitments and one null. Recursive all-parent availability
and an all-local/any-effective hybrid agreed on every supplied label; prior closure
and a conditional failed-move diagnostic also agreed on the available examples.
The successor needed mixed-depth references and an active pseudo-valid move that
failed to evade check. The recovery-only availability lesson was later retired;
the king-safety/retained-state distinction survives in the final foundation.

## Full anthology and full-course fit

The complete anthology work accounted for all source games, independently
compared notation and states, and reviewed artistic breadth. [Source qualification](../history/experiments.md#source-qualification)
and [curation](../history/experiments.md#source-qualification) explain why complete moves and
source membership remained distinct from presentation and metadata.

Shared anthology records reduced a 151,883-byte collection to
120,600 bytes, saving 31,283. The old replicated layout still missed capacity by
137 slots, before complete teaching. [That result](../history/experiments.md#recovery-and-capacity)
prevented a small move-stream estimate from being mistaken for full-product fit.

Clean-course fit required several compatible simplifications: a full-width
bootstrap strip, 6/6/3 move packing, shared literal history prefixes, and finally
direct length-delimited checked sections. The fragmented intermediate still
needed 2057 rows where only 1984 were available. The direct section layout
fit actual complete content at 495,353/507,904 payload bytes. Subsequent teaching
and final presentation refinements account for the final 506,509 bytes.
The [complete-fit analysis](../history/experiments.md#complete-fit) distinguishes
measured complete output from earlier estimates; see [decisions](decisions.md).

## Interpretation changed the teaching

Some failures were semantic rather than transport problems. Repeated specialized
king-safety scalar/row repairs still admitted wrong interpretations in p32–p35.
The recipient could produce plausible numbers while confusing side, focal piece,
ordinary failure and state retained after rejection. The [escape-context analysis](../history/experiments.md#king-safety-and-retained-state)
records those distinctions. Reusing the general attempted-move operation and
showing explicit both-side focal probes supported
[six finite p36 applications](../history/experiments.md#king-safety-and-retained-state).

The later integrated c0038 campaign closed **partial**, with a consequential
primary-presentation error and diagnostic-policy problems. Its old scores remain
unchanged. Prospective presentation/template corrections led to the scoped,
continued, source/own-work-assisted p44 composition.
The [partial disposition](../history/experiments.md#presentation-roles) and
[presentation audit](../history/experiments.md#presentation-roles) preserve both
the failure and the narrower later supported conclusion.

## The finished message and this repository

The first qualified local delivery was assembled and restored through offline reproduction. Later refinements changed the bootstrap, exact
piece masks and unused carried metadata. Revision labels remained external development
metadata; they are not hidden fields in the final square.

Actual raw reconstruction and verified local delivery established the [canonical final state](artifacts.md),
including the original PNG/TXT/BIN bytes.

The current repository retains the construction pipeline and headless mechanism
without the former browser aid. Earlier guided-interface
evidence remains history, not a newly supported product.
The [development findings](../history/experiments.md) summarize consequential
branches and their technical outcomes. They do not claim to expose a complete
replayable historical trial dataset.
