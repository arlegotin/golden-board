# Experiments and development findings

Golden Board developed through connected prototypes, transport alternatives,
source qualification, complete-course construction and model-based interpretation
checks. This account summarizes technical observations and their consequences.
It does not present an independently rerunnable historical trial dataset.

## Relay and passive teaching

A first interpretation interface returned one requested frame at a time. Basic
prerequisite inspection required many rounds before an assessment could begin.
That delivery method was stopped without a final interpretation assessment.
Its termination demonstrates a delivery problem, not recipient inability or
proof that the message lacked useful teaching.

Complete passive exports subsequently made the finite lesson routes available
in a batch. Three source-bound cases supported changed mobility, a blocked
opposite-side slider and geometric control versus legal movement in a pin.
These were finite, continued, prior-informed applications. They did not establish
complete practical chess, human learning or independent acquisition from scratch.

The final artifact retains passive instruction alongside interactive feedback.
A correct response is not required to gain access to the explanation.

## Recovery and capacity

Early transport prototypes used separated copies, normalization, conflict checks
and recovery instruction. Investigations found distinct failures in orientation,
conflict handling, checked availability, numeric windows and integrated assembly.
Partial methods did not automatically compose into a working complete method.
Later cooperative reconstruction did succeed, so the abandoned branch does not
establish that recovery was impossible.

Sharing anthology structures reduced 151,883 bytes to 120,600, saving 31,283.
The replicated carrier still exceeded capacity by 137 slots before complete
teaching was present. This exposed the difference between a small move stream
and a complete physical message with playback, teaching and transport overhead.

## Source qualification

All 64 input games were accounted for and retained. Strict parsing validates
standard-start main lines, complete token consumption, minimal SAN, matching
concluded scores and legal replay. Independent chess.js processing reads the
original SAN rather than the coordinates produced by the Python importer.
Every move and every-ply position is compared.

The reference comparison explicitly projects immediately recorded en-passant FEN
targets that the reference library may omit when they are unusable. This is a
representation convention, not the repetition rule: repetition includes an
en-passant square only when a legal capture exists.

Every source game stops on an active board. A recorded win or draw therefore
does not identify checkmate, resignation, agreement or the reason play stopped.
Canonical content ordering is separate from source order and descriptive metadata.
These distinctions remain enforced by the retained source and playback checks.

## Field windows and composed prerequisites

One prototype recovered all supplied data losslessly but still bound some values
to the wrong field windows. Correlated widths and zero edge bits admitted a shifted
interpretation; a reference began one cell too early. Correct values alone did not
establish the intended field boundaries. The remedy required explicit extents,
reference counts and independently varied values rather than repeated examples
with the same accidental correlations.

A foundation assessment later produced five exact answers and one null. Recursive
all-parent availability and an all-local/any-effective hybrid agreed on the
available labels. Prior closure and a conditional failed-move diagnostic also
agreed on the examples. Mixed-depth references and an active pseudo-valid move
that failed to evade check were needed to distinguish these alternatives.
Recovery-specific availability teaching was later retired; the distinction
between prior state and a rejected move's diagnostic survives in the foundation.

## Clean input scope

The final contract uses clean sampled cells or ordered, delimited symbols.
Damage recovery, replicas, voting and recovery-only teaching were retired while
all 64 complete games and the 2048-square maximum remained. Four recovery-only
obligations were removed, leaving 146. A superseded mixed checkpoint supplies
no passing result for the final contract.

The final CRC and inventory detect mismatches and incomplete content. They never
fill missing bytes, choose a plausible interpretation or repair a chess record.
Repeated examples and shared inert records teach or compress structure; they
are not transport copies for survival.

## Complete fit

Full-course construction combined a full-width bootstrap strip, packed 6/6/3 move
fields, shared literal history prefixes and direct checked sections. A fragmented
intermediate still required 2057 rows where only 1984 were available. Replacing
that framing with direct length-delimited sections fit the then-complete content
at 495,353 of 507,904 payload bytes.

Later teaching and presentation refinements produced the final 506,509 used bytes
and 1395 zero reserve bytes. No selected game was cut. The final capacity ledger
counts the bootstrap, inventory, five bodies, six section headers/checks and reserve.
Measured complete output, not a partial fixture, establishes this fit.

## King safety and retained state

Repeated specialized scalar/row repairs still admitted wrong interpretations of
side, focal piece, ordinary rejection and the state retained after rejection.
A hypothetical diagnostic can show why an attempted move fails while the actual
state remains unchanged. Those two pictures have different roles.

General attempted-move teaching and explicit both-side focal probes supported
six finite successor applications. This was a repair informed by specific rivals,
not a controlled comparison of teaching efficacy. A separate preceding assessment
had null answers caused by an underdetermined proof summary and missing phase
endpoints; those were different assessment/content ambiguities.

## Presentation roles

An integrated campaign closed partial despite many exact component results.
One primary presentation was interpreted incorrectly, and some diagnostic
predictions remained unsupported. A complete record, requested playback prefix
and most recent event board could not safely be treated as interchangeable.

A prospective presentation contract made those roles, full extents and operation
order explicit. Continued, source/own-work/template-assisted applications then
supported a composed finite interpretation. Earlier incorrect or null results
remain such; they are not retrospectively converted into passes.

The distinction matters beyond chess: a scalar cursor does not by itself prove
which whole record or event view was actually displayed. Literal operands,
complete frame extents and intermediate actions provide stronger evidence.

## Glyphs and grammar

The final piece alphabet contains twelve 5×5 masks: six roles for each side.
Opposite-side masks complement the meaningful 25 cells while unused padding stays
zero. Role bindings, counterexamples and a complete short record were checked
separately from inherited numeric interpretation. The public implementation and
literal regression masks make the exact palette inspectable.

The final grammar removes unused revision fields and metadata from carried
content. Development labels remain external. Only unused glyph definitions are
pruned; numeric zero values, visible masks, surviving IDs, moves, frames, feedback
and playback remain. A complete miniature demonstrates the resulting grammar.

## Final layout

The final square has 32 panels of 64×64 cells in one 2048×64 strip. Payload begins
at packed byte 16,384. The five document bodies carry representation, anthology,
foundation, practical and advanced material. Their combined body size is 506,181
bytes; inventory and six checked envelopes bring used payload to 506,509.
The total artifact is 524,288 packed bytes and exactly 4,194,304 cells.

Documentation crops preserve every panel cell. White gutters help the human guide;
no added corner labels enter those crops or the canonical message.

## Raw reconstruction

A submitted decoder for an earlier bootstrap layout was rerun unchanged with only each allowed observation and
a generic runtime, with repository, donor, prior-output and network access denied.
It recovered exact content in all 16 clean transform/polarity presentations and
the no-final-line-feed case, and refused five specified invalid inputs.

The method found a unique normalization and separator, derived the reflected
CRC polynomial from shift examples, tested checksum scope/initial/final conventions
and byte orders, and matched section boundaries and reserve. Independent checks
compared 32,249 record projections/locations, 773,976 original boundary bits and
469 animations containing 11,445 states, including all anthology states. Thus
matching final digests were accompanied by intermediate-method checks.

All transformation families and no-final-LF had already been selftested before
submission. Their reruns establish finite conformance, not unseen-family
performance. The method encodes the learned grammar; it is not a general unknown-
format inference system. Five refused malformed classes do not prove complete
hostile-input validation for that recipient program. Native strict validation is
checked separately by the current implementation.

## Byte bridge and interpretation

The five actual recovered bodies initialized the generic mechanism; developer
bodies served as comparison truth. Mechanical checks covered 4535 frames,
9123 successful actions, 757 atomic refusals, passive routes and 103 catalog roots.
These are mechanism observations, not a fresh recipient interpretation study.

Earlier interpretation was reused only through exact body, displayed/input/
prerequisite and mechanism equality, including 188 bindings across the 146
obligations. The composed result is source/own-work/template-assisted finite
application. Prior chess/geometric hypotheses, feedback and neutral routes were
available. This supports the bounded artifact-specific applications, not causal
learning or absence of all original outside assistance.

## Guided interface

A former browser aid displayed the exact artifact with modern labels. Mediated
model plans and finite browser actions checked display/control conformance.
They did not establish human usability or blind raw discovery. That interface is
outside the current repository; the generic headless mechanism remains.

## Reproduction and limits

The retained pipeline reproduces the exact final PNG, TXT, BIN and five bodies
from pinned source, package archives and qualified derivatives. Independent source
checks, literal vectors, every-ply playback and complete native checks protect
important boundaries where shared implementation assumptions could hide errors.

Original model/mode, complete outer tool history and exclusive original information
use remain unverified in parts of the historical demonstrations. Pretrained chess
knowledge was not erased. No result establishes human learning or retention,
universal comprehension, population success rates, arbitrary-format decoding,
radio acquisition, optical recovery or damaged-input restoration.

The [trial guide](../guide/trials.md) distinguishes information conditions and the
[build guide](../guide/build.md) explains what can be rerun from this source tree.
