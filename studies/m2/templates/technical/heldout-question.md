# Held-out observations

For each supplied held-out observation, apply the method you developed from the
clean artifact. Recover each input separately, preserving erasures and missing
units. Do not fill missing data from the clean observation or another result.
Report its artifact state, available streams and only those recovered facts
supported by checks in that observation. Complete content can be available
even when the artifact remains degraded. Do not force a recovery when the
correct conclusion is ambiguity, failure or a resource limit.
