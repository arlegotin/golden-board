Use the focused-checked, frozen trial package under
spec/m2-participant-trials-v1.md. Full release may follow the trial. Preserve
the exact files, expected results and conditions before exposure; a successful
blind result stays provisional until matching final automated checks pass.
An already released matching kit can also be used. Packaging alone is not a pass.

1. Give each member of the fixed two- or three-person group
   recipient/01-clean/. They can work asynchronously; no joint session or
   human-time limit is required. Keep later files withheld at first. Save
   code, commands, notes and actual outputs in each member's own answer folder.
2. When a member has a useful clean result and asks to continue, give that
   member recipient/02-adapter/. Keep each earlier version; a revised method
   is welcome and is judged by its actual evidence.
3. When that member asks for the observations, give recipient/03-heldouts/.
   Ask for separate results on a, b, c and d and the content query from
   actually recovered clean bytes. Other members may reach this stage later.
4. Let the group share its independently found methods and combine them.
   Give recipient/04-final-account/ and save one reproducible integrated
   account and final outputs. For each required part, one member's exact,
   observation-local solution suffices; the combined account must connect
   those parts into a complete recovery.

The expected outputs are for your comparison after the group has worked.
The optional export-format.md supplies conventions; if you give that help,
note it in the existing answers. Supplied conventions are not independent
discoveries. An incomplete but reproducible attempt is useful too.

No names, dates, timers, consent forms, eligibility questions or extra quiz.
