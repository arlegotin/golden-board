Use this kit after its fresh release check has passed.

1. Give the person recipient/01-clean/. Ask them to work out what the data
   conveys, using its own evidence. Time, wrong starts and backtracking are
   welcome. Save their working code, run command, notes and real outputs in
   answers/clean/ outside the input folder.
2. When they are ready, keep a copy of that submitted method. Give them
   recipient/02-adapter/. They can add a storage adapter; save it and its
   command in answers/adapter/, preserving the earlier method.
3. Give recipient/03-heldouts/. Ask them to run the saved method separately
   on a, b, c and d, and answer content-query.json from their actual clean
   recovered output. Save outputs and notes in answers/heldout/a/ through d/
   and answers/heldout/content-query/. Note any later interpretation changes.
4. Give recipient/04-final-account/. Save their account in
   answers/final-account.txt and collect the code, commands and actual outputs.

The expected outputs are for your comparison after the person has worked.
The optional export-format.md supplies conventions; if you give that help,
note it in the existing answers. Supplied conventions are not independent
discoveries. An incomplete but reproducible attempt is useful too.

No names, dates, timers, consent forms, eligibility questions or extra quiz.
