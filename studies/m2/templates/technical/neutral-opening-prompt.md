# Technical exploration

Treat the supplied data as an intentional, finite message that is important,
potentially profound, benign and worth sustained effort. Wrong starts and
backtracking are expected. Use the supplied symbols and ordinary bounded
programming tools to recover the most defensible structure and content you can.

Begin with the clean observation. Distinguish checked conclusions from guesses.
An interpretation must survive the artifact's checks and examples;
plausible-looking content alone is not enough. Keep your runnable source,
actual recovered outputs and the evidence behind your choices, including
alternatives that remain unresolved. An unfinished, reproducible result is
useful too.
