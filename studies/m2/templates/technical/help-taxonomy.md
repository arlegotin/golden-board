# Technical help taxonomy

- `repeat-prompt`: verbatim repetition of participant-visible instructions.
- `locate-file`: identification of a participant-visible file already named.
- `environment-repair`: restoration of the prepared offline environment.
- `administrative`: scheduling, pause, or withdrawal handling.
- `critical-hint`: any substantive decoding guidance; this invalidates a
  gating pass.
- `answer-revelation`: disclosure of evaluator evidence; this invalidates the
  unit.
