# Learner help taxonomy

- `repeat-prompt`: verbatim repetition of visible instructions.
- `locate-file`: identification of a participant-visible file already named.
- `environment-repair`: restoration of the supplied offline runner.
- `administrative`: pause, scheduling, or withdrawal handling.
- `critical-hint`: subject-matter or action guidance; invalidates a gating pass.
- `answer-revelation`: expected response or evaluator evidence; invalidates the
  unit.
