# M2 technical trajectory through trial 13

The recent technical rounds have entered a real loop. Earlier improvements in
clean discovery and content recovery are substantial, but damaged-recovery
composition has repeatedly remained unresolved after material teaching repairs.
The latest continuation localizes it more precisely; it does not discover a
new class of failure. Another isolated example followed immediately by another
fresh participant is not yet a justified prescription.

This retrospective changes no acceptance rule, historical result, frozen packet
or participant submission. M2 remains In progress. It does not authorize a new
participant handoff or imply that another repair will finish the milestone.

## Why this is a loop

Trial 10 already prescribed connecting physical IDs and inventory copy counts,
all lane/raw-repetition candidates, conflicts, completeness and availability.
It explicitly warned that additional numeric examples alone would not resolve
the mechanism. The next-work prescription after trial 13 substantially repeats
it. The omission was visible earlier: 05 named physical ownership, 06 separated
primitive execution from composition, and 08 demonstrated recovery with help.

Observation b is the strongest repeated operational symptom. Reviews of 11–13
find 127 complete objects in their limited recovery paths, against 135 expected
under the frozen procedure. The same eight recoverable sections are missing:
17, 18, 223, 224, 225, 246, 247 and 264. The first two use five copies and the
others two. This is repeated difficulty using copies whose independent decoding
fails, not unrelated chess or content problems.

These counts have different provenance: 11 freshly reran the unchanged assembler
on saved packet outputs; 12 includes post-checkpoint work; 13 reconstructed
missing programs before extending them. They are not three identical executions
of checkpointed decoders. Their shared omission is corroborated by submitted
implementations and continuations, not inferred solely from matching counts.

The repairs were material and narrower predictions did succeed. Trial 13 exposed
construction through ordinary examples. But more successful examples did not
demonstrate use of the complete procedure on observations. Its continuation's
exact fragment recovery is genuine progress inside the remaining boundary.

Earlier assistant descriptions of having "now" narrowed the cause overstated
novelty. "Closed in source" was explicitly limited, but moving directly from a
local repair to another fresh trial repeatedly treated that repair as stronger
readiness evidence than it provided.

## The trial may reinforce the omission

The [clean handout](../../artifacts/quiz/13-construction-technical-v2/packet/recipient/01-clean/READ-ME.txt)
asks what the data conveys and when the recipient is ready for more inputs.
It does not clearly explain there that readiness freezes substantive recovery
logic. The next handout says to keep the clean method unchanged. Section 16.7
of the [M2 specification](../../docs/m2-spec.md) intentionally withholds later
storage forms until that checkpoint to avoid discovery cues.

An intact carrier permits correct content recovery by decoded headers without
requiring raw-copy recovery. The current gate consequently tests whether the
recipient builds the complete recovery procedure before encountering its
necessity in a damaged observation. This is a more specific claim than eventual
defensible recovery by a persistent intelligent receiver.

That the framing contributes to the repeated omission is an inference, not
established participant psychology. The latest participant's
[account](../../artifacts/quiz/13-construction-technical-v2/answers-continue/answer.md)
also calls its explanation a retrospective inference: standalone examples and
successful clean decoding may have concealed the missing integration.

The checkpoint was predeclared and cannot be waived retrospectively. Task
wording alone also cannot repair the carrier. Review both the carried explanation
and whether the communicated task matches the scored claim. Explaining the
saved-method boundary in advance need not disclose the missing algorithm. Any
substantive change to information order or qualification must be prospective
and explicit in the owning specification.

## Credit the work without changing what it proves

Correct CRC-guided extra recovery is useful, not wrong-byte acceptance.
Autonomous post-checkpoint work is not automatically assisted work. It does not
establish the selected profile: candidate construction and assumptions differ
from its predeclared bounded procedure. Equivalent internal implementations are
allowed; the requirement is not to copy our code.

Physical ownership and conflict handling serve a concrete purpose. A damaged
or misleading but checksum-valid header must not decide where a copy belongs;
foreign bytes must not migrate into another group. Testing that protection is
reasonable. Whether complete pre-damage implementation is the right human
qualification boundary is a separate design question.

Do not add demands to interpret every opaque body, implement a general chess
engine, repeat correct selectors or explain every field label. The current
participant has completed the requested continuation; another small question
is unnecessary. This review infers neither population success rates nor
human/tool authorship from answer folders. No new administrative data is needed.

## Change the development decision before recruiting again

1. Preserve successful clean/content teaching. Treat the whole dependency as
   open: checked inventory → physical copies → all raw observations → candidate
   construction → conflicts/identity/state. Compare complete representations
   instead of automatically adding another local example. Replacing disconnected
   material may be better than accumulating examples. No evidence here justifies
   reducing the game count yet.
2. Use formative work, if useful with an already exposed willing collaborator,
   to develop and challenge this connection before another fresh trial. Look for
   application to a different small group, including an unreadable member header
   and conflicting evidence. Assistance is allowed in development and remains
   visible. This is not a new mandatory quiz or a substitute qualifying round.
3. Make the next prediction operational: derive the physical roster and complete
   decision from observations. Example counts and owner implementations alone
   cannot support that prediction. Review the readiness instruction alongside
   the design. Pre-checkpoint practice or eventual-recovery qualification would
   require a separate prospective protocol decision, not a silent rubric change.
4. A fresh trial should challenge a materially different, complete teaching
   approach. If the same omission returns, reconsider the representation or
   scope; do not rename another local patch a newly isolated root cause.
5. Keep the [human-first workflow](../../spec/m2-participant-trials-v1.md): focused
   checks and provisional feedback before expensive qualification. That reduces
   turnaround cost but does not by itself fix this reasoning loop. All final
   damage, native/Linux and release requirements remain.

The immediate next work is a design and task-alignment review using the existing
evidence, not another fresh participant handoff or exhaustive test run.
